Expected Firebase Data

The ESP32 publishes data under the /AKM SQUARE path in this format: 

{
  "temperature": 9.75,
  "temperature_status": "LOW",
  "current": -8.006,
  "current_status": "LOW",
  "vibration": "LOW",
  "motor": "RUNNING",
  "speed": "LOW"
}

The values above are examples only. Actual readings come from your hardware.

AKM Square Industrial Monitoring · Live data depends on your controller and Firebase configuration.
